# ObjectSpace Projection Architecture

## Overview

Projection transforms physical surfaces into intelligent workspaces.

Unlike traditional screen mirroring, projection is interactive, context-aware, and object-native.

ObjectSpace treats projection as a rendering technology rather than a display duplication feature.

# Design Philosophy

Traditional projection mirrors pixels.

ObjectSpace projects experiences.

The projected workspace remains fully interactive.

Objects continue to exist independently of the projection surface.

# Design Principles

• Projection renders experiences, not pixels.

• Objects remain independent of surfaces.

• Every surface may become a workspace.

• Projection is interactive.

• Sessions persist.

• Multiple devices cooperate.

• Projection is context-aware.

• Physical space becomes part of ObjectSpace.
