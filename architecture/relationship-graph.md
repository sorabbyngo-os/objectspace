# ObjectSpace Relationship Graph Architecture

## Overview

The Relationship Graph is the semantic backbone of ObjectSpace.

Traditional operating systems organize information using hierarchical directories.

ObjectSpace organizes information using relationships.

Everything becomes a node.

Everything may connect to everything else.

The graph enables AION to reason over knowledge rather than filesystem paths.

# Design Principles

• Relationships are first-class citizens.

• Objects exist independently of location.

• Meaning replaces hierarchy.

• Graphs replace rigid directories.

• Knowledge grows continuously.

• Traversal is semantic.

• Permissions are preserved.

• The graph is the foundation of intelligent retrieval.
